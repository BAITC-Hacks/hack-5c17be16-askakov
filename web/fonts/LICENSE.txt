Fonts bundled in this directory (subset to Latin + Cyrillic, static weights):

- Manrope (600, 700, 800) — designed by Mikhail Sharanda.
  https://github.com/sharanda/manrope
- IBM Plex Mono (400, 600) — designed by IBM / Bold Monday.
  https://github.com/IBM/plex

Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
